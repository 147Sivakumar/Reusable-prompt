Chain-of-Thought style — Branding Product
Product: Organic skincare moisturizer

Prompt example
Act as a professional brand strategist. We are launching a new organic facial moisturizer for women and men aged 20–40 who prefer natural, sustainable skincare.

Develop the brand step by step:

Identify the target customer's needs and pain points.

Define the brand positioning.

Identify the product's unique selling proposition.

Develop the brand personality and tone of voice.

Generate 5 possible brand names.

Select the strongest name and briefly explain the selection criteria.

Create a tagline.

Recommend a color palette and visual style.

Create a short brand story.

At each stage, briefly explain the key considerations behind the decision and use the result from the previous stage to inform the next one.

Flow
Customer → Positioning → USP → Personality → Name → Tagline → Visual Identity → Brand Story
