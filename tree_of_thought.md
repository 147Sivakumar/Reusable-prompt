Tree-of-Thoughts style — Branding Product
Here, instead of following only one path, you ask the model to explore multiple branding directions, compare them, and select the strongest one.

Prompt example
Act as a senior branding consultant. We are launching a premium organic facial moisturizer for consumers aged 20–40 who value natural ingredients, sustainability, and premium skincare.

Explore three different branding directions:

Direction A — Natural & Sustainable

Develop a brand positioning.

Suggest 3 brand names.

Create a tagline.

Suggest a visual style.

Direction B — Premium & Minimal

Develop a brand positioning.

Suggest 3 brand names.

Create a tagline.

Suggest a visual style.

Direction C — Modern & Youthful

Develop a brand positioning.

Suggest 3 brand names.

Create a tagline.

Suggest a visual style.

Then evaluate all three directions using these criteria:

Target audience appeal

Differentiation

Memorability

Premium perception

Sustainability perception

Potential for future product expansion

Score each direction from 1–10 for every criterion.

Select the strongest direction based on the evaluation.

Finally, develop the selected direction into:

Final brand name

Brand positioning

USP

Tagline

Brand personality

Color palette

Packaging concept

Brand story

Briefly explain why the selected direction is stronger than the alternatives.
